Information value by candidate feature

IV = Σ (%good − %bad) × WoE on the coarse bins, train 2013–14. Below 0.02 is dropped; all are "weak" (< 0.1) because this data has no FICO score.

Logistic regression on WoE features

Statsmodels Logit. With WoE = ln(%good/%bad), every coefficient should be negative. All final features are, and all are significant.

Scorecard points

PDO 20, 600 points at 50:1 good:bad odds. An applicant's score is the sum of one bin's points per feature; higher scores mean lower PD.

Score distribution, train vs 2015

Bands are train deciles. PSI < 0.10 is stable, 0.10–0.25 monitor, > 0.25 significant shift.

Feature stability (CSI)

PSI computed on each scorecard feature's bins. All are far below 0.10, so the applicants didn't change; their outcomes did.

Realised LGD on defaulted loans

Train 2013–14 defaults. LGD = 1 − recoveries ÷ principal outstanding. Most recover 10–15% (charged-off debt is sold); a large group recovers nothing.

LGD by grade, 2015 defaults

Actual LGD is flat across grades, so borrower data at origination says little about recovery.

Share of loan outstanding at default, 2015

EAD ratio = (funded − principal repaid) ÷ funded. Riskier grades default earlier, with more still owed.

Models vs simple baselines, 2015 defaults

The two-stage LGD model barely beats a flat average. EAD's R² comes mostly from loan size.

Data: Lending Club loans issued 2013–2015 (Kaggle "Lending Club Loan Data"), individual 36-month loans with a resolved outcome. Amounts in US dollars. Train = 2013–14 issues, out-of-time test = 2015 issues. Every figure is generated by run_all.py and embedded by build_site.py; see the README for method, assumptions and limitations. A portfolio project, not a bank-approved model.
